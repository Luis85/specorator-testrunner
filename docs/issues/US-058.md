# US-058 Flakiness score & quarantine

## User Story

As a **QA Engineer**, I want scenarios flagged flaky (status flips,
retry-passes) and a quarantine workflow with owner and fix-by date, so that
flakiness is managed instead of eroding trust.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Stability score per scenario over the history window
- `@quarantine` tag excludes from KPI (like `@wip`) but still runs
- Quarantine note records owner + deadline
- Dashboard shows quarantined count and oldest deadline
- Quarantined >cap (default 5% of scenarios) raises a warning

## Progress

- **Slice 1 — flakiness scoring projection (done):** the foundational "stability
  score per scenario over the history window" AC. Pure domain projection
  `computeFlakiness` (pass↔fail flip rate + classification bands) over the US-057
  history window, exposed via `ScenarioHistoryService.flakiness()`.
  Design: [flakiness scoring](../superpowers/specs/2026-06-17-us-058-flakiness-scoring-design.md).
- **Slice 2 — `@quarantine` tag (done):** scenario-level KPI exclusion — a
  quarantined scenario is dropped from the Use Case roll-up (still runs and
  records history), plus a visible quarantined count on the per-Feature health
  line and `@quarantine` seeded into the tag vocabulary.
  Design: [quarantine tag](../superpowers/specs/2026-06-19-us-058-quarantine-tag-design.md).
- **Still to do:** quarantine note recording owner + fix-by deadline; dashboard
  quarantined count across the UC & oldest deadline; cap warning (default 5%);
  retry-pass signal via the Cucumber Messages parser.
