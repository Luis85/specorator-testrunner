# UC-031 Evaluate release readiness and record sign-off

> Compute a GO/NO-GO verdict from configurable thresholds and record the
> decision as a durable sign-off note.

- **Domain:** Evidence
- **Primary Actor:** Product Owner
- **Epic:** [[EPIC-015]] — Audit-Grade Evidence & Release Readiness
- **Stories:** [[US-062]], [[US-063]]
- **Owning Service:** to be assigned at acceptance

## Preconditions

- Readiness thresholds configured (min pass rate, max quarantined, zero
  failing on `@critical`, evidence present) — sensible defaults apply.
- Recent runs and per-scenario history exist for the release scope.

## Main Success Scenario

1. Product Owner opens the release-readiness view.
2. Plugin computes the verdict with a per-threshold breakdown; every number
   drills down to its underlying scenarios/runs.
3. Product Owner reviews any failing threshold via drill-down.
4. Product Owner selects "Record sign-off".
5. Plugin generates the sign-off note: scope, readiness snapshot, deferred
   defects, named approver, decision field, timestamp.
6. Product Owner records the decision in the note in Obsidian.
7. The note links the readiness snapshot and Evidence; git history provides
   the audit trail.

## Extensions

- **2a. Verdict is NO-GO:** sign-off can still be recorded — as a rejection,
  or as a GO-with-exceptions whose justification is part of the durable
  record. Defensibility, not gatekeeping, is the product goal.

## Postconditions

- A durable, attributable approval record exists in the vault instead of an
  email thread.

## Domain Events

Proposed (finalized with the EPIC-015 work):

- `readiness.evaluated`
- `release.signedOff`
