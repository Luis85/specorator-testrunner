# UC-033 Assemble an audit evidence bundle

> Export a date range of evidence notes, the traceability matrix snapshot,
> and referenced artifacts as one indexed folder/zip.

- **Domain:** Evidence
- **Primary Actor:** Business Analyst
- **Epic:** [[EPIC-015]] — Audit-Grade Evidence & Release Readiness
- **Stories:** [[US-065]]
- **Owning Service:** to be assigned at acceptance

## Preconditions

- Evidence notes carry audit-grade stamps ([[US-060]]): timestamps, commit
  SHA, environment, runner/browser versions.

## Main Success Scenario

1. Business Analyst selects "Export audit bundle".
2. Business Analyst picks the date range.
3. Plugin gathers the range's Evidence notes (Markdown), a traceability
   matrix snapshot, and the referenced artifacts.
4. Plugin writes one folder/zip with an index note listing contents and
   their hashes.
5. Business Analyst hands the bundle to the auditor — no screenshot
   scramble.

## Extensions

- **3a. A referenced artifact no longer exists** (retention swept): the
  index records the gap explicitly so the bundle's completeness is honest.

## Postconditions

- A self-contained, hash-indexed bundle exists; the vault is unchanged
  apart from the export location.

## Domain Events

Proposed (finalized with the EPIC-015 work):

- `audit.bundleCreated`
